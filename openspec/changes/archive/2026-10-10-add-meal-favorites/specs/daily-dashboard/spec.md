## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mark favorites from Home
Home SHALL show each listed meal's favorite state and SHALL let the user toggle it from the list.

#### Scenario: Star a meal
- **WHEN** the user marks a meal on Home as a favorite
- **THEN** the meal shows as a favorite on Home and becomes available to re-log from the capture screen

#### Scenario: Unstar a meal
- **WHEN** the user unmarks a favorite meal on Home
- **THEN** the meal shows as not a favorite and is no longer offered for re-logging
